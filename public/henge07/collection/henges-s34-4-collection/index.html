Collection S34/4

Discover the Henge’s brand new S34/4 Collection – Unwritten Streets.

Some streets aren’t marked on maps. They live in the material, they whisper through the light, they surface at the precise moment when design meets intuition.

The 2025 Collection is an invitation to walk one of these invisible paths.

An unprecedented journey, where everything arises from the tension between form and function, between artisanal mastery and visionary design, between past and possibility.

In this new chapter of our journey, we collaborate with voices that amplify the Henge DNA: the architectural subtlety of Tanju Özelgin, the chromatic and luminous experimentation of Johanna Grawunder, and the organic fluidity of Yabu Pushelberg.

Their vision intertwines with our longstanding lead designers — Massimo Castagna, Ugo Cacciatori, and Isabella Genovese — continuing to rewrite, together, the meaning of material.

Each piece in the Henge’s S34/4 Collection is a silent statement. A deliberate gesture. A rare balance between aesthetic boldness and timeless refinement.

Murano glass and steel merge into luminous vibrations. Oversized sculpted stones trace soft geometries. Architectural systems, onyx, rare woods, burnished metals and iridescent reflections. Everything speaks the language of those who can listen beyond appearances.

Henge does not simply present furniture. It creates interior geographies. Spaces to be first inhabited by the gaze, and only then by the body. Objects designed to transcend time. To accompany those who live by instinct, not rules.

Download the S34/4 Catalogue and immerse yourself in the complete universe of the Unwriteen Streets collection, where every detail narrates a story of visionary design, artisanal excellence, and timeless elegance. Explore the dialogue between past and possibility, form and intuition, matter and light.

Continue the journey in the world of Henge, visiting the catalogues page and the collections page to discover the brand’s previous collections, each one a chapter in an ever-evolving design language.
